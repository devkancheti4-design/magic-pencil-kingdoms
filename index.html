How it works

  • Draw on the paper with the left mouse button or a finger. Pan with the right button, arrow keys, the mouse wheel, or the minimap.
  • Name it – the name is the wish. archer shoots, horse runs, wall blocks, tower guards, bomb explodes, cake lures roaches, doctor heals.
  • Only inside your kingdoms (green ground) can drawings come alive. Your army marches to the next enemy castle. Smash it to capture the kingdom.
  • Shape matters – bigger = tougher, more legs = faster, spikes = damage, closed loops = armour, more detail = masterpiece bonus.
  • Studio – take all the time you want: pen for outlines, bucket or crayon to colour in (like the magic pen in the cartoon), mirror, undo. Save designs to your library and stamp them into the world as many times as you like.
✎ Design Studio – the world is paused. Take your time.

Magic Pencil: Kingdoms of Paper

You found a magic pencil. Whatever you draw and name comes alive – just like the cartoon. The cockroaches found one too and scribbled five kingdoms across the paper. Take them back.

Draw inside your kingdom → name it (that's its wish) → it comes alive and marches on the next castle. Its shape decides its strength; its name decides what it does. Ink is unlimited – your imagination is the only limit.

Try: archer, knight, fast horse, 3 arrowmen guard, fire dragon, wall, tower, bomb, cake, doctor, army. Open the Studio to design a masterpiece.

Shape rules

SizeBigger → more HP and damage, slightly slower.
LegsVertical lines reaching the bottom count as legs. 2 legs normal, 4 faster, 6+ very fast. No legs → wriggles at 65% speed.
SpikesSharp zig-zag corners add damage (+6% each, up to 10).
Closed loopA closed body outline gives +25% HP (armour).
DetailMasterpieces with many strokes get up to +50% HP. Designing pays off.
Colouring inA bucket-filled body counts as a closed body (+25% HP armour).
