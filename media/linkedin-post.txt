I built a game where whatever you draw comes alive. 🖍️

Remember the Oggy and the Cockroaches episode with the magic pen? Draw a monster, name it "giant strong green monster", and it stomps off to war. Give an archer 4 arms and it fires 4 arrows at once. Write "floating blades" and it floats… and spins.

Magic Pencil: Kingdoms of Paper
• Draw anything. Name it. The name is its wish, the shape is its strength.
• Unlimited ink. Six kingdoms to capture. A design studio where you can spend hours on a masterpiece.
• Free, open source, runs in your browser – no download.

And the part I'm most excited about: your creations vs. everyone else's. Add your creature to the GitHub repo and it invades other players' kingdoms as an unknown challenger.
