A fun little side project I've been tinkering with: a browser game where whatever you draw comes alive. 🖍️

It's inspired by the Oggy and the Cockroaches "Magic Pen" episode. You draw something, give it a name, and it walks off to do what its name says. "4 arm archer" fires four arrows. "Floating blades" floats and spins. "Giant strong green monster" does what you'd expect.

It's free, open source and runs in the browser. There's a small studio for drawing your own creatures, and the part I'd love help with: creatures other people add to the repo show up in the game as challengers, so you end up facing everyone else's ideas.

If you enjoy games, drawing or JavaScript and feel like contributing a creature, a word or a fix, the door is open. Feedback of any kind is welcome too. Still rough around the edges, but it's fun.
